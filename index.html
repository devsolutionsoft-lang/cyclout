About

Cyclout is a personal page created by Mohamed Baali, a runner and mountain biking enthusiast based in Agadir, Morocco 🇲🇦.

In recent years, I started cycling and exploring the hidden roads, mountain trails, valleys, and beautiful landscapes around Agadir and beyond.

What began as a hobby has grown into a real passion for outdoor adventures and discovering new places.

This pages created as a way to share my cycling journeys, running adventures, photos, and videos, and to showcase the beauty of Morocco through my eyes.
